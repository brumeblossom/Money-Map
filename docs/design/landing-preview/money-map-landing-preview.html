Why Money Map

What you need to know about Money Map

Money Map is a simple money diary for your phone and laptop. Record what comes in, what goes out, what you give and who owes you, in seconds. Then see exactly where your money went each month.

It's built for everyday people, not accountants. There's no bank login and no complicated budget to set up. Your records stay private to you, and the app keeps working when your network is poor.

Is Money Map free?

Yes. Money Map is free to use. You don't need a card to sign up.

Do I need internet to use it?

No. You can record entries without internet. They're saved on your device and back up to your account automatically when you're online again.

Is my money information safe?

Yes. Only you can see your records when you're signed in. Money Map never asks for your bank details or connects to your bank, and we don't sell your data.

Does it connect to my bank account?

No. You add entries yourself, in seconds. That keeps things private and works with cash, transfers and anything else.

Can I use it on my phone and laptop?

Yes. Sign in with the same account on both and your records match everywhere. On your phone you can also add Money Map to your Home Screen like an app.

Can I try it without an account?

Yes. Tap "Try without an account" and start recording. Your entries stay on that device only. Create an account any time to back them up.
